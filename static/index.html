    Help, Privacy & Honest Limits

    Storage

    Where your data lives

    Your profile, answers, lesson progress, and Memory cards stay in your local SQLite database on this device. Export or restore a backup any time in Progress → Backup & Data.

    Voice

    How voice works

    Prepped never stores raw audio recordings. Depending on your setup, transcription runs on-device (Vosk) or via Groq Whisper—the active mode is always shown in the voice privacy badge.

    Limits

    What feedback can and cannot judge

    Feedback checks your words against transparent, rule-based checklists. It does not score accent, pronunciation, grammar mastery, or placement readiness.

    Shortcuts

    Keyboard navigation

    Press Ctrl K (or Cmd K) to search across Prepped, and Esc to close dialogs. Text input is always available alongside voice.
