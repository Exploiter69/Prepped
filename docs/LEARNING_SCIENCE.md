# Learning Science & Evidence-Based Curriculum Design

Prepped's curriculum (46 sequenced lessons, 155 exercises, 14 structured units across 3 progression stages, plus 36 Career Mock prompts and a 4/3/2 Oral Fluency Drill) is grounded in peer-reviewed Second Language Acquisition (SLA) and cognitive psychology research rather than passive video watching or gamified streaks.

## Core Scientific Methods Embedded in Prepped

| Method | Primary Research | How Prepped Applies It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Four Strands** | Nation, P. (2007). *Innovation in Language Learning and Teaching*, 1(1), 2–13. | Balances **Meaning-Focused Input** (listening dialogues & interview cues), **Meaning-Focused Output** (pushed speaking/writing), **Language-Focused Learning** (targeted grammar, connectors, collocations), and **Fluency Development** (timed repetition on familiar content). |
| **The Output Hypothesis** | Swain, M. (1985, 1995, 2005); Izumi (2002). | Every lesson ends in active production (`short_response`, `reflection`, or `pronunciation`), forcing syntactic processing and helping learners notice exact gaps instead of relying on passive comprehension. |
| **Retrieval Practice (Testing Effect)** | Roediger, H. L., & Karpicke, J. D. (2006). *Psychological Science*, 17(3), 249–255; Dunlosky et al. (2013). *PSPI*, 14(1), 4–58. | Replaces passive note re-reading with active recall (`choice`, `ordering`, error repair, and open explanation) paired with immediate explanatory feedback. |
| **Distributed Practice (Spacing)** | Cepeda, N. J., Pashler, H., Vul, E., Wixted, J. T., & Rohrer, D. (2006). *Psychological Bulletin*, 132(3), 354–380. | Pairs lessons and feedback with Prepped's local spaced-review **Memory** deck (`Save a learning note…`) and progressive prerequisites across Foundation → Developing → Placement-Ready stages. |
| **4/3/2 Fluency Proceduralization** | Nation (1989); de Jong, N., & Perfetti, C. A. (2011). *Language Learning*, 61(2), 533–568. | Implemented directly in **Career → Timed Mock → 4/3/2 Fluency Drill (90s → 60s → 45s)** and taught in *Build oral fluency with the 4/3/2 method*. Repeating the same explanation under shrinking time limits proceduralizes reusable speech frames that transfer to new topics. |
| **The Intelligibility Principle** | Levis, J. M. (2005). *TESOL Quarterly*. | Targets high-functional-load clarity features—word stress, thought-group chunking, and crisp `-ed`/`-s` word endings—rather than accent imitation or native-speaker bias. |
| **Self-Explanation & Structured Framing** | Dunlosky et al. (2013); STAR/CAR & BLUF engineering communication frameworks. | Sequenced units train Constraint → Choice → Trade-off reasoning, 20/60/20 STAR behavioural stories, daily standups, constructive code reviews, and live coding narration. |

## Sequenced Progression Map (46 Lessons · 14 Units)

1. **English Foundations (6 units · 24 lessons):** Sentence Foundations (4) → Grammar in Context (4) → Vocabulary in Context (4) → Listening for Details & Interview Cues (4) → Speaking Fluency & 4/3/2 (4) → Intelligibility & Pronunciation (4).
2. **Project & Workplace Communication (4 units · 12 lessons):** Project Explanation Structure (3) → Evidence, Trade-offs & Debugging (3) → Workplace Engineering Communication (3) → Stakeholders, Scope & Live Demos (3).
3. **Placement & Interview Mastery (5 units · 15 lessons, sharing the 2 foundational project units):** Project Structure (3) → Evidence & Trade-offs (3) → HR & Behavioural Mastery (3) → Technical & CS Explanation (3) → Recovery, Follow-Ups & Closing (3).
4. **Starting Point Snapshot (1 unit · 1 diagnostic lesson):** Unscored baseline snapshot (`baseline-check`).

## Epistemic Honesty

All curriculum stages (`foundation`, `developing`, `placement-ready`) describe **lesson sequence**, never a measured learner CEFR level or placement guarantee. Independent human educator sign-off remains flagged (`human_review_required: true`) in the content metadata.
